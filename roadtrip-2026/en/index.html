04 / LEAVE ROOM FOR WINTER

Head west. Let the weather decide.

High elevations in November · Check daily
!

Check the rental’s traction-device policy

Before booking, confirm the vehicle, tires and permission to use chains or other traction devices required on the route with the chosen rental company. Caltrans R2 controls exempt qualifying four-wheel-drive vehicles with the required snow tires from installing chains, but they must still carry traction devices; R3 requires installation on every vehicle. Don’t assume an SUV, AWD or ordinary all-season tires guarantee access. Confirm the vehicle, tires and contract at pickup. If the requirements cannot be met, wait in a town or change plans.

A

Wyoming: wind, snow and closures

Before leaving Omaha on Nov 24, check the Cheyenne–Laramie–Rawlins–Rock Springs corridor. If roads close or travel is discouraged, wait in a town with hotels and supplies, such as Cheyenne or Laramie. Don’t bypass closure gates, improvise on back roads or assume I-70 is safe.

Use the Nov 26 Salt Lake City rest day to absorb delays first.

Sierra: cross the pass in daylight

On Nov 28, before reaching Reno, check the next day’s I-80 / Donner Summit outlook. If the 28th clearly offers a better window, cross early and stay in Sacramento only if well rested and with enough daylight. Otherwise stay in Reno, skip sightseeing and wait for safe access.

Nov 29 is the planned arrival day, with no extra wrap-up day. A pass delay means adjusting arrival and rental-return plans.

C

Prolonged bad weather: change plans

Start checking corridor forecasts 72–48 hours before departure, then every evening and before driving. For a multiday storm, reassess the dates, a substantial reroute or the mode of travel. A southern route is not a quick detour or a guarantee of snow-free roads.

If Nov 29 becomes unrealistic, contact the rental company before the return deadline, adjust onward plans and revise the budget.
